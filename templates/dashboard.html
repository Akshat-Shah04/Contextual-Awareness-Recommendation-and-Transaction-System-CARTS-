CARTS vs Apriori: hold-out evaluation

80% of baskets train the rules. For each of up to 1,500 held-out baskets one item is hidden, and each strategy recommends 5 items from the rest of the basket.

Scoring formula

Score(i) = [ Σr∈R(i) (wssr + wccr + wlℓr + wvvr) ] × (αpPn + αmMn + αfFn + αrRn)
  • R(i): rules A → i that fire for the cart
  • s, c, ℓ, v: support, confidence, lift, conviction
  • P, M, F, R: profit per unit, margin %, purchase frequency, revenue of item i
  • Each metric is Z-scored, then squashed to (0, 1) with a logistic function
  • Apriori baseline: best firing rule's support × confidence
